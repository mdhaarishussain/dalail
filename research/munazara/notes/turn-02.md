### 3. Counter-Questions Used by Ahl-e-Sunnat

- CQ-06 (Semantic scope of du'a in Quran):
  Summary: Asks how the opponent reconciles equating every vocative call with worship when the Quran uses d-'-w for the Prophet ﷺ calling believers (3:153), believers calling each other (24:63), and Ibrahim calling dead birds (2:260).
  Used in: yt-hqDt_1BwzLc#M07, yt-hqDt_1BwzLc#M09.

- CQ-07 (Physical posture analogy):
  Summary: Asks why physical postures (standing, sitting on knees) are evaluated by intention and relationship (worship in namaz vs respect for parents or teachers), but verbal calling is treated as intrinsic worship regardless of belief.
  Used in: yt-hqDt_1BwzLc#M10.

- CQ-08 (Geographic consistency on shirk):
  Summary: Asks how calling unseen beings for assistance can be permitted in a wilderness/desert but classified as shirk in a town or city, asking whether location can alter the theological status of an action.
  Used in: yt-3-tML7NbATk#M13.

- CQ-09 (Non-physical assistance in Quran 27:40):
  Summary: Asks whether Asif ibn Barkhiya bringing the throne of Bilqis before the blink of an eye was accomplished through physical means (ma taht al-asbab) or spiritual power granted by Allah (ma fawq al-asbab).
  Used in: yt-hqDt_1BwzLc#M04, yt-vMA-Vcng63I#M03.
