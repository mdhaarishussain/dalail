### New Counter-Question Themes Registered

- CQ-33 (Bukhari-Muslim Exclusivity / Urinating Standing & Drinking Standing): If an action is only Sunnah if found in Sahih Bukhari and Muslim, why does Bukhari contain 4 narrations of the Prophet urinating standing and zero of sitting, while the Ummah acts upon Aisha's narration in Tirmidhi (12) that he only urinated sitting? And why does Bukhari contain 3 narrations of the Prophet drinking while standing, while Sahih Muslim strictly forbids it? If text presence in Bukhari does not make standing urination mandatory Sunnah, why impose this arbitrary filter on prayer?

- CQ-34 (Turnabout on Imam Malik & al-Mudawwanah / 4 Raf al-Yadayn Consensus Myth): When opponents claim that all 4 Sunni Madhhabs and all sects agree on 4 raf al-yadayn except Hanafis, why do they conceal the explicit ruling of Imam Malik in his primary foundational work al-Mudawwanah al-Kubra (1/118): "I do not know of raising hands in any part of prayer... and raising hands according to Malik is da'eef except at takbeerat al-ihram"?

- CQ-35 (Narration Counting vs Naskh / The 26 Mut'ah Hadiths in Sahih Muslim): If a legal ruling is decided simply by which position has more numerous narrations, why does the Ummah unanimously prohibit Mut'ah (temporary marriage) when Sahih Muslim contains 26 narrations permitting it (3410–3435) and far fewer prohibiting it? If earlier permissible narrations were abrogated (mansookh) regardless of their count, why dismiss abrogation (naskh) in the case of raf al-yadayn?
