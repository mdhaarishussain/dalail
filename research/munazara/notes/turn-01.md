### 3. New Claims Inventory (Engineer Muhammad Ali Mirza)

- EM-C01: Invoking absent or deceased figures verbally (Ya Rasool Allah madad, Ya Ali madad) constitutes du'a, and since du'a is worship (ad-du'a huwal ibadah), any such call directed at creation is shirk. (Sources: yt-hqDt_1BwzLc, yt-3-tML7NbATk, yt-0LqCS-zvCxY).

- EM-C02: The hadith of calling unseen beings (Ya Ibad Allah A'inuni) applies strictly to lost travelers in desolated wilderness/jungles (referring to terrestrial angels), and cannot be generalized to calling upon prophets or saints in cities or homes. (Source: yt-3-tML7NbATk).

- EM-C03: Waseela is categorized into exactly five scriptural forms (living person's du'a, righteous deeds, Divine Names, relics/tabarrukat, and praying for the heavenly rank Al-Waseela); invoking persons after death is unlegislated. (Source: yt-sAVly9evIpA).

- EM-C04: Even if Allah had granted authority (ikhtiyar) to prophets or saints, calling on them remains shirk because the righteous figures themselves only ever called upon Allah (Iyyaaka Nasta'een). (Source: yt-0LqCS-zvCxY).

### 4. Counter-Questions Used by Ahl-e-Sunnat (Not in CQ-01–CQ-05)

- CQ-06 (The Semantic Proof from General Vocatives in the Quran):
  Concept: If every act of calling (nida / du'a) is worship (ibadah), why does the Quran use the root d-'-w for the Prophet ﷺ calling the believers (wa rasoolu yad'ookum in 3:153), the believers calling one another (ka-du'ai ba'dikum ba'da in 24:63), and Ibrahim calling dead birds (thumma ud'uhunna in 2:260)?
  Force: Compels the opponent to admit that du'a linguistically means calling/summoning, and that calling becomes worship only when the addressee is called with the belief of divinity (uluhiyyah).

- CQ-07 (The Physical Posture Analogy):
  Concept: Standing (qiyam) and sitting on knees (qa'dah) are acts of worship in Salah, yet standing for parents/teachers or sitting on knees before a teacher (Hadith Jibril) is universally accepted as honor (ta'zeem). Why is a physical posture evaluated by intent and context, but calling (nida) treated as intrinsic worship regardless of belief?
  Force: Exposes the flaw of equating external form with intrinsic worship.

- CQ-08 (Geographic Restriction Inconsistency):
  Concept: If calling upon unseen created beings in the wilderness (Ya Ibad Allah a'inuni) is permitted by hadith, how can calling upon unseen created beings in a city be shirk? Can geographic location transform shirk into tawhid?
  Force: Demonstrates that if an action is intrinsic shirk, it cannot be permissible in any location.
