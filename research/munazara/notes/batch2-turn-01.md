### 3. New Opponent Claims Discovered

- NEW-01 — Physical Wall vs Global Hearing Paradox: Opponents argue that since the Prophet could not hear behind an ordinary wall during earthly life, asserting that he hears calls from across the globe after death is an ungrounded contradiction (yt-sq2CPVTwIT0, M13).

- NEW-02 — Fatawa Alamgiri Marriage Witness Objection: Opponents cite Hanafi fiqh compendia stating that naming the Prophet as a witness to a marriage is kufr because he lacks knowledge of unseen affairs (yt--Donk1omKUs, M04).

- NEW-03 — Conveyance and Active Intervention Burden: Opponents demand explicit textual proof that supplications directed to a deceased martyr are delivered to him and that he has capacity/permission to travel and grant aid (yt-SvTDDbl1uYw, M03).

- NEW-04 — Sanctity of Hujra Aisha Matn Objection: Opponents object to the Malik al-Dar narration by arguing that an unnamed non-mahram man could not enter Aisha's private living quarters during her lifetime without violating purdah rules (yt-VwfdF6s7SiY, M05).

- NEW-05 — Dereliction of Duty Matn Objection on Umar: Opponents argue that the Prophet's dream statement to Umar (al-kayyis al-kayyis) implies negligence in governance, which violates Umar's uprightness (yt-VwfdF6s7SiY, M06).

- NEW-06 — Ontological Division of Ma Taht vs Ma Fawq al-Asbab: Opponents assert that any request directed to creation beyond natural physical causality (ma fawq al-asbab) is inherently shirk (yt-L1jmqPI02aE, M08).

### 4. Counter-Questions Used by Defending Debaters (Not in CQ-01–CQ-05)

- CQ-06 — The Tashahhud Direct Address Dilemma: Every Muslim addresses the Prophet directly in daily prayer (As-salamu 'alayka ayyuhan-Nabiyyu). If direct vocative address (nida) to the physically absent or deceased Prophet is shirk, how can salah contain it? (yt-L1jmqPI02aE, M07).

- CQ-07 — The Angel Jibreel Addressing Dilemma: When Umar converted to Islam, Angel Jibreel descended and called out Ya Muhammad! (Sunan Ibn Majah 108). If using ya for the Prophet is worship or shirk, was Jibreel committing shirk? (yt--Donk1omKUs, M06).

- CQ-08 — The Bedouin at the Grave (Utbi Narration) Dilemma: Classical mufassirin (including Ibn Kathir) cite the Utbi narration under Surah An-Nisa 64 where a Bedouin came to the Prophet's grave after his passing, asked for his intercession, and was forgiven. If seeking waseela after death was considered shirk or bid'ah by the early generations, why did classical imams cite it approvingly? (yt-sq2CPVTwIT0, M05).
