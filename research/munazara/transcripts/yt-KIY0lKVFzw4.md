notes: "Mufti Rashid Mahmood Razvi refutes Engineer Muhammad Ali Mirza and Ghair-Muqallid polemicists regarding the manner of descending into prostration (sujud). Opponents claim that placing knees before hands violates the hadith 'Do not kneel as a camel kneels' (Sunan Abi Dawud). Razvi shows that physically a camel places its front limbs (hands) down first, cites Ibn al-Qayyim in Zad al-Ma'ad establishing the hadith as 'Maqloob' (inverted text), cites Imam al-Tahawi's correct transmission in Sharh Ma'ani al-Athar, and presents three authentic narrations proving knees before hands: Wa'il ibn Hujr (Tirmidhi 268), Anas ibn Malik (Mustadrak 822), and Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas (Sahih Ibn Khuzaymah proving hands-first was abrogated), corroborated by contemporary Saudi scholars including Shaykh Salih al-Fawzan and the practice of the Imams of the Haramain."
