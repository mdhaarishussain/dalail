notes: "Demonstrates the religious legitimacy and recommendation of reciting the Qur'an at the graves of deceased Muslims. Cites consensus on the dead benefiting from supplication (du'a) and charity, referencing Imam at-Tahawi's 'Aqidah and al-Khadimi's commentary. Quotes Imam ash-Shafi'i (al-Umm), Imam an-Nawawi (al-Majmu', Sharh Sahih Muslim), and al-Qurtubi's deduction from the hadith of the two moist palm branches (jaridatayn). Analyzes hadiths instructing recitation over the dead ('iqra'u 'ala mawtakum surata yasin'), refuting the claim that 'mawtakum' only means the dying. Details Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal's famous retraction from censuring recitation at the grave after being informed of 'Abdullah ibn 'Umar's will to recite Surat al-Baqarah at his burial (narrated by al-Khallal in al-Amr bi-l-Ma'ruf)."
